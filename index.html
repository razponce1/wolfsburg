Ask a question
What services does Wolfsburg offer?

Technical services for pumps and mechanical seals: repair and maintenance, troubleshooting and root cause failure analysis, upgrades and parts supply, and specialized on-site services such as technical supervision, service maintenance contracts and acid tank cleaning.

Which types of pumps can you repair?

Overhung, between-bearings, vertical and vacuum pumps, with proper assembly and tolerances. We also handle shaft alignment, installation and commissioning.

Do you provide on-site support?

Yes. We offer on-site technical supervision for pumps and mechanical seals, service maintenance contracts, and acid tank cleaning and services.

What special metals can you supply?

Through voestalpine Specialty Metals Asia Pacific: bars, billets, machined parts, sheets, plates, tubes, piping, wires and boiler tubes in Titanium, Hastelloy, Inconel, Monel, Duplex, Zirconium and Tantalum. Additive manufacturing and 3D printing are also available.
